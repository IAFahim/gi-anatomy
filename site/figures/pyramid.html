Fig 2 · how queries never scan

The pyramid

A field of a million cells is not searched. It is descended: root, node, tile, cell — 80 numbers and one page, and the answer is the same number all the way down.

What the figure shows

  • The root plate carries the 16 live node slots of a 1024² field. The pyramid's array is 8×8 wide; only 16 of its slots are in use at this size.
  • The node plate carries the 64 tile slots of one node — a node covers 8×8 tiles, which is 256×256 cells.
  • The tile plate carries the 1024 cells of one tile, 32×32 int16, published as one 2048 B page.
  • The bead is the maximum on its way down. Every level holds the same number, because a parent is the maximum of its children.

The descent is modelled from Gi's own Pyramid<T> (fan-out 8) and MaxQueries.Visit: the root's 16 live slots, then the node's 64 tile slots, then one page. Numbers come from build/model/pyramid.mjs, which deposits the same kernels Gi does.
